Run by a drug store (or a Walmart, just bleach your soul afterwards) and get the following: 

![PXL_20260807_201216698.jpg](/uploads/PXL_20260807_201216698.jpg)

* Toothbrush- extra soft, for brushing out hackle, fur and dubbing to make it buggier. 
* Shitty scissors- Not pictured but you can imagine what a pair looks like.  For cutting wire and other synthetics.  You can also just find a pair in your house or (eventually) use an old pair of tying scissors. 
* Sally Hansens Hard As Nails- I use this for head cement.  With a good whip finish, it's good enough to keep the fly wrapped up for longer than it takes me to lose it. 
* Reading Glasses [optional]- If you're an old, like me, throw a pair into your tying kit so you can better see the flaws in what you tie.
