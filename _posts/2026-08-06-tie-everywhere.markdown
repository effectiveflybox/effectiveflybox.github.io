I think it's real important to have a place where you can have materials prepped and ready to go on the hook.  If it's easy to tie a fly, you're probably going to tie a fly.

This weekend, I am a couple hours north of home in the mountains.  Because I put together a minimal set of tools and materials, the whole tying area can come with me.  With a couple of trips coming up, I will knock down a dozen chubby chernobyls while I am here.
