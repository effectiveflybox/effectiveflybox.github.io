If you come back to this site in a year from now, this section may be different.  Today, right now, I find using synthetic hair to be easier to tie with than natural hair such as deer or elk hair.   

For my dry flies (and the bigger terrestrial I tie), I use Enrico Puglesi synthetic hair.  This stuff is really cool.  It's uniform so you don't have spend a lot of time prepping it.  It comes in a range of colors (to the extent that's important) and it floats great.  Takes flotant well too. 

As important, it forms a really nice wing on dry flies.  What's not to like?  Well, it's synthetic and therefore it's another plastic that's potentially going into the environment.  I suspect, I'm going to start trending away from this and convert to using natural materials here. 

No judgement though, I think using barbless flies is a more impactful change than using synthetic materials.  Still, I sure do put a lot of flies into trees above and beneath the waterline.  Maybe I should be more mindful of what is attached to those hooks.
